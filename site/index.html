NOBUY watches every new SOL-paired pump.fun coin and decides at second ten. It can't spend anything on its own. Every buy and every payment goes to review, and the review answers ALLOWED or BLOCKED, in plain English. Red card: never. Yellow card: not past the limit.

Questions

Does the agent make money?

We don't claim it does, and we never will. Our own backtest says simple buy rules lose after fees. The agent trades on paper so you can watch the review work on real launches without anyone's money at risk.

Then what's the point?

Most trading losses aren't bad luck. They're specific, avoidable trades: buying after the dev sold, buying from a factory, revenge-trading, sending to the wrong address. The review blocks those. Keeping your bankroll is the part of trading you actually control.

Will it catch every rug?

No. It blocks the specific shapes listed above, which are checkable on-chain at second ten. A dev can still sell after you buy. When that happens, the agent's exit rule is "dev sold, out". Read the rules, not the hype.

Do I need to connect a wallet?

No. The page is read-only. Nothing to sign, nothing to approve.

Is this live trading?

No. Paper mode. The agent refuses to start in live mode. Every paper trade pays pump.fun's fees both ways, so the numbers aren't flattered. The x402 data payments are real USDC.

What is x402?

A way for software to pay for a web request as it makes it: HTTP status 402, "Payment Required", settled in USDC. It lets an agent buy exactly one data call instead of a monthly plan. It also means agents will get targeted by the same address tricks people do, which is why every payment goes to review.

Can I run the review on my own bot or wallet?

Not yet. The rules and limits are public, so you can copy them into your own trading today. If we ship a version for your own agent, it'll be on this page first. No date promised.
